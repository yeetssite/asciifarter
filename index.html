What is AsciiFarter?

AsciiFarter is a simple program that pulls ascii art out of it's ass(our Ascii database) and prints it into nearly anything. The concept of Asciifarter in it's full glory allows you to print any funny or random Ascii art, anywhere, any time(unless it's, like, a social media platform you're banned from, AsciiFarter can't help you with that).

AsciiFarter is also not just a computer program, but an idea of a computer program. It's not a program for the users, it's a program for the developers, which would normally make it a pretty shitty program for the person using it, but that isn't the point.

The goal of AsciiFarter is to create a program that doesn't care what programming language it's coded in, that doesn't give a fuck how much RAM is installed in your $2 Temu PC, and will actually congratulate you if you backwards engineer it or modify the source code.

AsciiFarter's plan(yes, AsciiFarter is now a person, be nice to him) is to teach people that it doesn't matter what programming language you use, what specs your PC has. If you have enough of a will to make something, and enough of a want to make something you think is fuckin' awesome. Then, with enough thought and effort, and especially care put into it, you can make that thing. And it doesn't matter if nobody else sees that thing, as long as you see it, and you think it's cool. That's all that matters

AsciiFarter isn't just gonna be a Python script, it's gonna be JavaScript, a C++ program, written in BrainFuck, hell, screw that, coded in pure binary, none of that Assembly shit(if you knew ball, you'd understand). AsciiFarter is whatever the hell AsciiFarter wants to be, and if you don't like that, then you're just a Big ol' Meanie.

As long as AsciiFarter's main goal of farting Ascii from the art database is acheived, it doesn't matter who makes it or how it's made, it's AsciiFarter.
